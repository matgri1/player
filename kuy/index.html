About us

What is Kuy?

Kuy was born in Quy Nhon. We live here, know the city inside out, and built the services we ourselves were missing when we first arrived.

Instead of hunting for a dozen contacts across group chats, you get one trusted team that takes care of the everyday things — so you can enjoy the sea, the food and the city.
